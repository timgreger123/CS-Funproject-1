Quick summary: Our Diesel Budget Planner helps trucking companies calculate their monthly diesel costs and profits, compare different price scenarios, and estimate next week’s diesel price using historical data and machine learning.

`data/diesel_history.csv` contains weekly EU diesel prices including taxes.
  The published prices were converted from EUR per 1,000 litres to EUR per litre.
  [European Commission, Weekly Oil Bulletin](https://energy.ec.europa.eu/data-and-analysis/weekly-oil-bulletin_en)

`data/diesel_history_ch.csv` contains monthly Swiss diesel prices including
  taxes, in CHF per litre.
  [Swiss Federal Statistical Office, table su-d-05.02.91](https://statistik.admin.ch/de/asset/36970598)

  `data/source_metadata.json` records the EU dataset's source, retrieval
  time, units, and conversion.
